# 04 — Compound Relational Architecture

**Status:** implemented candidate architecture with conventional physical admission gates.

## 1. Why composition is not a state

Resonant Chemistry does not identify a molecular formula with one unique relational topology. The current architecture separates

```text
composition -> candidate relational states -> physical admission
```

A composition can therefore carry several model-defined states before conventional calculations distinguish among them.

## 4. Admission boundary

A successful geometry optimization is not by itself a harmonic local minimum. Therefore v0.14A1 cannot establish:

- local-minimum character without a Hessian/frequency gate;
- a global or ground-state ranking;
- a bonding/topology label from geometry alone;
- a TIR relation operator from numerical survival alone.

Only after the complete frozen 45-start execution is durably persisted may a later Hessian/local-minimum gate be preregistered.
