\section{Correlated spin--orbit rediagonalization in the period-2 active space}
\label{sec:period2-correlated-spin-orbit}

The active-space CI layer of Sec.~\ref{sec:period2-active-ci} improves the electrostatic term centers without refitting the radial Slater integrals.  The next question is whether the previously derived atom-specific spin--orbit scale can be rediagonalized inside the same correlated space without introducing a new empirical parameter.

\subsection{Conservative projection}
The established period-2 spectroscopy control layer already supplies an atom-specific Pauli central-field constant $\zeta_{2p}$.  Version 0.1 reuses that same constant and projects the one-electron operator
\begin{equation}
H_{\mathrm{SO}}=\zeta_{2p}\sum_i \mathbf l_i\cdot\mathbf s_i
\end{equation}
onto the lowest radial $p$ channel of the two-radial-orbital CI basis.  The virtual radial $p$ channel and cross-radial spin--orbit blocks are set to zero.  This is deliberately conservative: no virtual-channel $\zeta$ is inferred from experiment, and no cross-radial scale is introduced by hand.

The correlated Hamiltonian is therefore
\begin{equation}
H_{\mathrm{corr,SO}}=H_{\mathrm{active\,CI}}+P_{2p}H_{\mathrm{SO}}P_{2p},
\end{equation}
with $P_{2p}$ the projector onto the physical lowest radial $p$ channel.  The full determinant-space Hamiltonian is rediagonalized and classified by $J^2$, with $L^2$ and $S^2$ retained as approximate labels.

\subsection{Frozen benchmark replay}
Experimental values were already frozen by the earlier post-hoc NIST audit.  They are not consumed by the Hamiltonian.  Relative to the previous equivalent-shell fine-structure control, the correlated calculation gives

\hline
Target & previous / cm$^{-1}$ & correlated / cm$^{-1}$ & frozen reference / cm$^{-1}$\\
\hline
B $^2P_{3/2}$ & 14.859 & 14.859 & 15.287\\
C $^3P_1$ & 19.157 & 18.219 & 16.417\\
C $^3P_2$ & 57.213 & 54.410 & 43.413\\
O $^3P_1$ & 177.496 & 144.907 & 158.265\\

F $^2P_{1/2}$ & 464.719 & 383.480 & 404.141\\

B is preserved, while every listed C, O, and F target improves.  The strongest reduction in relative error occurs for O $^3P_0$ and F $^2P_{1/2}$.

\subsection{Rejected stronger ansatz}
A prototype in which the physical $2p$ spin--orbit scale was extended to the virtual radial channel through a radial $\langle r^{-3}\rangle$ scaling produced excessive quenching of several splittings.  That ansatz is therefore not promoted.  The present projector construction keeps the validated physical $2p$ scale and leaves virtual/cross-radial spin--orbit coupling open for a future derivation.

\subsection{Verification and boundary}
The exact repository workflow on GitHub Actions runs both the active-CI and correlated-spectrum tests.  At the current checkpoint all seven tests pass.  This validates implementation and numerical regression, not high-precision spectroscopy.  Core--valence correlation, additional radial $p$ orbitals, $s/d$ virtual channels, virtual spin--orbit matrix elements, Dirac self-consistency, and Breit terms remain open.

TIR and affective mappings remain absent from the control Hamiltonian.
