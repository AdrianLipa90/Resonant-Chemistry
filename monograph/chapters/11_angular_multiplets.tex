\section{Angular Multiplets: From Configuration Average to LS Structure}

The H--Kr control solver establishes a common radial/electrostatic baseline, but an open shell is not fully specified by a configuration label such as $2p^2$ or $3d^7$.  The next layer resolves the allowed electrostatic LS multiplets without modifying the already verified control energies.

\subsection{Microstate construction}

For an equivalent-electron shell with orbital angular momentum $l$, the one-electron spin-orbital set is
\[
\{|l m \sigma\rangle: m=-l,\ldots,l;\;\sigma=\pm\tfrac12\}.
\]
For $N$ electrons the antisymmetric determinant basis therefore contains
\[
\binom{2(2l+1)}{N}
\]
microstates.  The implementation constructs the two-electron Coulomb operator directly in this determinant basis.  Angular Coulomb matrix elements are evaluated from the spherical multipole expansion and integer Wigner $3j$ symbols computed by the Racah factorial formula.  Radial Slater parameters $F^k$ remain explicit coefficients.

The many-electron operators $L^2$ and $S^2$ are constructed independently from second-quantized one-body ladder operators.  Each degenerate electrostatic eigenspace is subsequently diagonalized with respect to $L^2$ and $S^2$.  Therefore the term label $^{2S+1}L$ is an output of the angular algebra rather than a hard-coded periodic-table label.

\subsection{Equivalent $p$ shells}

With the dimensionless diagnostic choice $F^0=0$, $F^2=1$, the solver obtains
\[
p^1\to{}^2P,\quad
p^2\to{}^3P,\quad
p^3\to{}^4S,\quad
p^4\to{}^3P,\quad
p^5\to{}^2P,\quad
p^6\to{}^1S.
\]
For $p^2$, all 15 microstates are resolved into
\[
{}^3P\;(9),\qquad{}^1D\;(5),\qquad{}^1S\;(1),
\]
where parentheses give degeneracy.  For $p^3$, all 20 microstates become
\[
{}^4S\;(4),\qquad{}^2D\;(10),\qquad{}^2P\;(6).
\]
The same equivalent-shell algebra applies to both the $2p$ sequence B--Ne and the $4p$ sequence Ga--Kr.  Spin--orbit splitting into $J$ levels is intentionally outside this layer.

\subsection{Equivalent $d$ shells}

For a dimensionless angular diagnostic $F^0=0$, $F^2=1$, $F^4/F^2=0.625$, the ground-term sequence is
\[
{}^2D,\ {}^3F,\ {}^4F,\ {}^5D,\ {}^6S,\ {}^5D,\ {}^4F,\ {}^3F,\ {}^2D,\ {}^1S
\]
for $d^1$ through $d^{10}$.  In particular, $d^5$ contains $\binom{10}{5}=252$ microstates and its lowest angular term is $^6S$.

This is an angular control result.  The ratio $F^4/F^2=0.625$ is not fitted to any atom-specific spectrum; atom-specific term splittings require coupling these angular coefficients to radial Slater integrals extracted from the converged atomic state.

\subsection{Status and next gate}

This layer promotes equivalent-shell LS structure from OPEN to IMPLEMENTED/TESTED for $p$ shells and to a tested angular control diagnostic for isolated $d$ shells.  It does not yet claim fine-structure $J$ splitting, explicit $3d$--$4s$ exchange for chromium, or atom-specific multiplet separation in Hartree/eV.  Those are the next gates.

TIR and affective mappings remain absent by construction.  Multiplet invariants may later become semantic inputs, but they are not allowed to rewrite the control physics.
