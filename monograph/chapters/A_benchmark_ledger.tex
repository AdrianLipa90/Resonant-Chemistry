\section{Interpretation rule}
A benchmark status is local to the declared model and gate.  For example, H--Kr coverage does not imply high-precision term-resolved spectroscopy for every element, and a passing shell-topology unit test does not validate a topological law of chemistry.  The most restrictive scientifically relevant caveat accompanies the result throughout the book.

\section{Current open numerical frontier}
The most developed correlation branch ends at a clear diagnostic boundary: no tested fixed frozen-orbital carbon truncation balances both $^1D$ and $^1S$ simultaneously.  The next required control is state-averaged orbital optimization/MCSCF over a common $^3P$, $^1D$, $^1S$ objective without experimental term-energy fitting.  This gate is independent of the shell-topology candidate and should be completed even if the topological hypothesis later fails.
