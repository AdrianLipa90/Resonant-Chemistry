\section{Interpretation rule}
A benchmark status is local to the declared model and gate.  H--Kr coverage does not imply high-precision term-resolved spectroscopy for every element, a passing shell-topology unit test does not validate a topological law of chemistry, and a successful molecular relaxation does not establish a harmonic minimum or electronic topology.  The most restrictive scientifically relevant caveat accompanies the result throughout the book.

A missing execution receipt is not a failed chemical hypothesis.  Conversely, a complete software workflow does not promote a scientific candidate.  Runtime cancellation, SCF failure, optimizer failure, negative controls and falsifiers are retained as distinct status classes.

\section{Current correlation frontier}
The first one-coordinate state-averaged active--external carbon gate has produced a strict decrease in its frozen equal-weight state-average objective and is therefore variationally informative along the tested direction.  The next correlation control is multidimensional state-averaged orbital optimization/MCSCF-type relaxation without experimental term-energy fitting, followed by correlated spin--orbit re-evaluation only after that orbital layer is frozen.
