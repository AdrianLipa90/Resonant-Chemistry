\chapter{From Nuclear Centres to Molecular Shell Dynamics}
\label{ch:multicentre-shells}

\section{Why the nucleus must remain in the model}
A shell topology detached from its nuclei would discard the dominant attractive structure that organizes ordinary atomic and molecular bound states.  The multicentre extension therefore starts from the standard clamped-nuclei electronic Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
H_e=\sum_{i=1}^{N_e}\left[-\frac12\nabla_i^2-\sum_{A=1}^{M}\frac{Z_A}{|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf R_A|}\right]
+\sum_{i<j}\frac{1}{|\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j|}
+V_{NN},
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
V_{NN}=\sum_{A<B}\frac{Z_A Z_B}{|\mathbf R_A-\mathbf R_B|}.
\end{equation}
The nuclei $\mathcal A_A=(Z_A,\mathbf R_A)$ are attractive Coulomb centres for the electrons.  In the exact conservative Hamiltonian they are not dissipative attractors; in an iterative electronic-structure algorithm, however, a converged fixed point may possess a basin of attraction in the numerical state space.  The two meanings are kept distinct.

\section{Shells as local organization, bonding as global reorganization}
An isolated-atom configuration provides a useful local shell decomposition, but a molecular eigenstate is not generally a tensor product of immutable atomic shells.  Bond formation changes the global one-particle density matrix, orbital occupations, exchange structure and correlation.  The shell-level programme therefore uses atomic subshells as incoming coordinates and asks how their control observables reorganize when the nuclear-centre set changes from
\begin{equation}
\{\mathcal A_A\}_{\rm separated}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\{\mathcal A_A\}_{\rm molecule}.
\end{equation}
A bond is not defined here as a knot.  The control definition remains energetic and electronic: a molecular state is characterized by its Hamiltonian, density, reduced density matrices and response to nuclear geometry.  Topological descriptors may be appended only as derived diagnostics.

\section{Multicentre winding diagnostics}
For a declared oriented projection $P:\mathbb R^3\rightarrow\mathbb C$, an electron path may be measured relative to each nuclear centre,
\begin{equation}
w_{iA}[\gamma]=\frac{1}{2\pi}\oint_\gamma d\arg P(\mathbf r_i-\mathbf R_A),
\end{equation}
and relative electron paths may be measured by
\begin{equation}
w_{ij}[\gamma]=\frac{1}{2\pi}\oint_\gamma d\arg P(\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j).
\end{equation}
For $M>1$ nuclei, the vector $\mathbf w_i=(w_{i1},\ldots,w_{iM})$ can distinguish projected circulation around different centres.  These quantities are projection-dependent unless an effective two-dimensional physical constraint is separately justified.

\section{Basin transfer as a reduced diagnostic}
In a reduced orbital or density-flow description, one may define centre-weighted populations using a fixed partition of real space, for example
\begin{equation}
Q_A=\int_{\Omega_A}\rho(\mathbf r)\,d^3r,
\end{equation}
with the partitioning rule specified independently of the desired chemical conclusion.  A bond-formation path then induces a redistribution
\begin{equation}
\Delta\mathbf Q=(\Delta Q_1,\ldots,\Delta Q_M).
\end{equation}
A future topological descriptor may be compared with this redistribution, but it cannot replace charge conservation or create a bond where the control wavefunction does not support one.

\section{Adiabatic and nonadiabatic paths}
For a nuclear path $\mathbf R_A(\lambda)$, an adiabatic electronic branch $|\Psi(\lambda)\rangle$ may acquire a geometric phase
\begin{equation}
\Gamma=i\oint\langle\Psi(\lambda)|\partial_\lambda\Psi(\lambda)\rangle\,d\lambda.
\end{equation}
This is conceptually distinct from projected particle winding.  Any combined shell-topology record must therefore keep Berry/Pancharatnam holonomy, exchange parity and projected spatial winding as separate fields rather than collapse them into one scalar.

\section{Candidate molecular state record}
A minimal multicentre extension of Chapter~\ref{ch:shell-nbody-topology} is
\begin{equation}
\Sigma_{\rm mol}=\left(\{Z_A,\mathbf R_A\},\rho,\gamma,\{n_k\},\pi_{\rm exch},\{w_{iA}\},\{w_{ij}\},\Gamma,\mathcal P_{\rm shell}\right),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal P_{\rm shell}$ records the chosen shell/subshell projection and its provenance.  The density $\rho$, one-body density matrix $\gamma$ and natural occupations $n_k$ are control observables; the winding and conformal fields are candidate diagnostics.

\section{From one structural candidate to a competing-state ensemble}
The compound trajectory exposed a structural problem that a single molecular graph cannot represent safely.  The same stoichiometry may support qualitatively different electronic or intermolecular realizations.  In particular, the closed-shell $XY_2$ study contains an activated linear three-centre/four-electron candidate and weak $X\cdots Y_2$ complexes under the same empirical formula.

Version v0.13 therefore replaces the mapping
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{composition}\longrightarrow\mathrm{one\ predicted\ graph}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{composition}\longrightarrow\mathrm{unranked\ state\ ensemble}
\longrightarrow\mathrm{physical\ admission}.
\end{equation}
For each of the nine frozen Ne/Ar/Kr $\times$ F/Cl/Br compositions, the current ensemble contains
\begin{enumerate}
\item \code{ACTIVATED\_LINEAR\_3C4E},
\item \code{WEAK\_COMPLEX\_LINEAR\_END\_ON},
\item \code{WEAK\_COMPLEX\_T\_SHAPED}.
\end{enumerate}
This produces 27 state candidates.  Their prior rank and prior probability are explicitly absent.  Enumeration is not a stability prediction, and reference knowledge about KrF$_2$ or rare-gas--halogen van-der-Waals complexes is not converted into a prior score.

\section{First common molecular-state relaxation control: v0.14A/A1}
The first executable consumer of the v0.13 ensemble is a conventional low-cost relaxation screen.  It is not a new Resonant-Chemistry Hamiltonian.  The frozen electronic-structure policy uses B97M-V with VV10 nonlocal correlation, the def2-TZVPD basis, neutral singlets, PySCF and geomeTRIC, and one common SCF/optimizer policy.

The original preregistration used a common numerical-grid policy.  A backend smoke then exposed a PySCF SG-1 pruning limitation while constructing the KrF$_2$ nonlocal-correlation grid.  No KrF$_2$ energy had yet been produced.  The v0.14A1 amendment therefore changed only the pruning algorithm, globally for both regular and nonlocal grids, to \code{nwchem\_prune}.  The functional, basis, grid sizes, charge/spin policy, optimizer, SCF thresholds and seed geometries remained frozen.  This amendment is retained separately rather than rewritten into the original preregistration.

The amended backend smoke passed, and F$_2$, Cl$_2$ and Br$_2$ were then relaxed from the same 2.0~\AA{} starting separation.  Their same-method equilibrium distances are used only as numerical seed scales.  Each $XY_2$ composition receives five frozen starts: three symmetric activated-linear starts with $r_{XY}/r_{YY}=1.0,1.3,1.6$, one weak end-on start, and one weak T-shaped start.  The complete preregistered matrix therefore contains 45 $XY_2$ relaxations.

\section{Partial v0.14A1 execution evidence}
The first full workflow was cancelled after the common backend and ligand-dimer stages and after eight of the nine formula jobs had produced receipts.  Those first-attempt formula receipts were recovered and persisted unchanged.  The durable checkpoint is therefore
\begin{equation}
8/9\ \mathrm{formulae},\qquad 40/45\ \mathrm{frozen\ starts}.
\end{equation}
The missing composition is ArBr$_2$.  Its status is \code{MISSING\_EXECUTION}; no chemical negative label is inferred from cancellation or missing runtime evidence.

For all eight completed compositions, the lowest successful \emph{screening} electronic energy belongs to one of the weak-complex seed families.  Because no Hessian has been evaluated, this does not establish a ground state or even harmonic local-minimum status.

The activated-start survival pattern itself is retained as a threshold-free diagnostic.  Representative observations are:
\begin{itemize}
\item KrF$_2$: three of three activated starts complete and converge to the same stored-precision symmetric basin, with $r_{\rm Kr-F}\approx1.883$~\AA; this basin lies about $9.288$~kcal/mol above the lowest successful weak-linear screening state under the same method;
\item ArF$_2$: three of three activated starts complete, with an activated-to-lowest-weak screening gap of about $37.99$~kcal/mol;
\item NeF$_2$: two of three activated starts complete, with a corresponding gap of about $95.93$~kcal/mol;
\item NeCl$_2$ and NeBr$_2$: none of the three activated starts complete, while both weak seed families complete;
\item ArCl$_2$, KrCl$_2$ and KrBr$_2$ retain respectively two, two and one successful activated starts out of three.
\end{itemize}
These values are descriptive screen outputs only.  No activation law, threshold, topology classifier or seed-specific rescue rule is fitted from them.

A later rerun request re-executed a broader GitHub matrix scope than intended.  To preserve provenance, the original first-attempt receipts remain the v0.14A1 evidence for the eight already-completed formulae.  Only a future ArBr$_2$ receipt may fill the missing slot without replacing those first-attempt records.

\section{Execution partition frontier: v0.14A2}
The missing ArBr$_2$ slot exposed an execution-unit problem rather than a need for a new molecular model.  In v0.14A1 all five frozen starts for one formula were evaluated sequentially inside one formula job, so cancellation before final serialization could erase already-performed work.  Version v0.14A2 therefore changes only the execution partition:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{one\ frozen\ seed}\longrightarrow\mathrm{one\ process}\longrightarrow\mathrm{one\ durable\ receipt},
\end{equation}
followed by deterministic reconstruction of the existing five-start formula receipt.  The frozen functional, basis, grids, SCF thresholds, optimizer thresholds, five seed rules and no-rescue policy are unchanged.

The execution surface is exposed through the repository command
\begin{equation}
\code{phase\ chem\ seed-manifest},\quad
\code{phase\ chem\ relax},\quad
\code{phase\ chem\ aggregate}.
\end{equation}
Each execution unit receives a deterministic 36-component address derived from the frozen method-policy hash and seed-identity hash.  This address is explicitly model-defined routing/provenance metadata and is \emph{not} a measured molecular phase space or a physical holonomy observable.

A further reproducibility constraint is required.  Re-running the Br$_2$ dimer optimization under nominally identical settings changed the optimized distance at approximately the $10^{-12}$~\AA{} level.  That variation is chemically negligible but changes the exact serialized seed coordinates and therefore their identity hashes.  v0.14A2 consequently reuses the exact first-attempt v0.14A1 Br$_2$ receipt bytes instead of reoptimizing the dimer.  The frozen source is workflow run \code{31795895258}, artifact \code{9217426658}, raw \code{Br2.json} SHA-256
\begin{equation}
\code{6c322bbc7ea31cfb51e4d195fcaeea32747e09447cd1dd56a8aab4771af19602},
\end{equation}
with $r_{YY}=2.2842324866344543$~\AA{}.  The reuse policy is \code{EXACT\_BYTES\_NO\_REOPTIMIZATION}.  The frozen method-policy hash is
\begin{equation}
\code{9b58d32d361bd2c2248c10859b12785aa9aa0244e18b5504f6cbb79d2abe366d}.
\end{equation}
Both values are hard-gated before a backend calculation may start.

The five ArBr$_2$ execution-unit cards and their canonical seed hashes are registered before execution.  They remain epistemically \code{PENDING} until durable receipts are persisted.  A parent-side timeout is written as \code{EXECUTION\_TIMEOUT\_UNKNOWN}; SCF or optimizer failure is retained as an executed backend result; neither status is translated into a chemical falsification.  v0.14A2 therefore remains an execution frontier and does not by itself promote the scientific checkpoint beyond v0.14A1.

\section{Electronic topology is a separate admission problem}
A linear symmetric geometry is not by itself evidence for a three-centre/four-electron electronic topology, just as an $XY_2$ formula is not such evidence.  The v0.10 admission contract therefore keeps three independent diagnostic families separate: orbital-subspace evidence, real-space/force evidence, and fragmentation/isomer-energy evidence.  A topology label requires independent agreement under that contract; insufficient evidence remains UNKNOWN.

The v0.14A/A1 relaxation screen supplies candidate geometries and energies to later controls, but it does not replace v0.10.

\section{Simple-system molecular validation ladder}
Simple systems remain necessary controls even though they are no longer the first molecular code implemented in the repository:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\mathrm{H}_2^+$ as a one-electron two-centre control;
\item $\mathrm{H}_2$ as the first exchange/correlation bond;
\item HeH$^+$ as an asymmetric two-centre test;
\item LiH as a core--valence separation test;
\item only then more complicated multi-centre or ring systems where loop descriptors are especially vulnerable to confirmation bias.
\end{enumerate}
This control ladder is complementary to the v0.14 closed-shell $XY_2$ screening trajectory rather than its historical predecessor in implementation order.

\section{Current status and next gate}
The repository now contains an implemented conventional molecular-state relaxation layer, partial v0.14A1 execution evidence, and an implemented v0.14A2 exact per-seed recovery path for the missing ArBr$_2$ formula.  It still does \emph{not} contain a validated Resonant-Chemistry molecular topology or molecular-energy law.

Until all five exact ArBr$_2$ execution-unit receipts are durably persisted and audited, the admitted scientific checkpoint remains 8/9 formulae and 40/45 frozen starts.  Only after all nine formula receipts and all 45 frozen starts are durably present may a separate v0.14B Hessian/local-minimum protocol be frozen.  A timeout, cancellation, SCF failure or optimizer failure remains explicit evidence and does not license target-specific rescue.

After local-minimum admission, conventional energetic comparison and the independent v0.10 electronic-topology diagnostics may be applied.  TIR, PhaseNav and affective semantics remain outside the control electronic energies unless a separately defined no-refit observable passes its own admission gate.
